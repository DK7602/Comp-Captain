COMP CAPTAIN - HOW TO INSTALL
=============================

This version is M4 (0.5.0): takes, lyrics, scores and the comp. Record takes with CAPTURE, add audio files, or bring
in the takes on a Cubase track's lanes (ARA). Every take lands on its bar. Give it your lyrics and it listens to your
takes and lines every line up, so the teleprompter follows your song. Every line of every take gets scores; hear any
take from Comp Captain, pick, and Build Performance joins the best lines into one take you drag into Cubase.
Tightening and the Export page come next (they still show a sample song, marked SAMPLE). On your track it passes your
audio through untouched (unless you ask it to play a take or the comp).

The "Comp Captain.vst3" folder is bigger now (about 60 MB): it carries the speech model that listens to your takes,
on your computer (nothing is sent anywhere). Copy the whole folder, as before.

WINDOWS (Cubase and other DAWs)
1. Close your DAW.
2. Copy the folder "Comp Captain.vst3" into:  C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
   (Windows may ask for permission: click Continue.)
3. Open your DAW. In Cubase, if it doesn't show up, open Studio > VST Plug-in Manager and click Rescan.
4. Put Comp Captain on a vocal track as an insert effect, then open it.
   "Comp Captain.exe" is the standalone version: double-click it to try it without a DAW. (It borrows the speech
   model from the installed VST3, so copy the VST3 first.)

MAC
1. Copy "Comp Captain.vst3" into  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
   and "Comp Captain.component" into  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
2. Open your DAW and rescan plug-ins if needed.
   (These test builds are not signed yet: if macOS blocks it, right-click > Open, or allow it in
   System Settings > Privacy & Security.)

LINUX
Copy "Comp Captain.vst3" into  ~/.vst3

TRY M4 IN CUBASE (the test for this version)
Use your project with the takes you already recorded (Comp Captain on the track, lyrics in, takes listened to).
1. Comp page, Line view. Press the play button (triangle) next to a take: you hear that take of the line, straight
   from Comp Captain, with Cubase stopped. Try a few takes of a line. (If nothing plays, turn on the track's
   Monitor button, the speaker.)
2. Pick the takes you want, like last time (click a take). Try the Picky-ness slider (right): towards Fewer cuts
   the comp keeps one take going when two are close.
3. Press the big play button at the top: the comp plays from the line on screen and the lyric follows along.
   Listen to the joins between lines: any clicks, jumps in volume, or cut-off words?
4. Press Play in Cubase, then A/B: you hear the comp in time with your beat, in place of your track. Press A/B
   again to hear your track as it is.
5. Press BUILD PERFORMANCE (gold button). When the Assistant says the file is ready, hold the mouse down on
   "Drag to DAW" and drag it onto a new audio track in Cubase, at the bar it says (with Snap on).
6. Play it in Cubase (mute the original track): does it sound like one performance, in time with the beat?
   Send what you heard, especially any join that doesn't sound right (which line).

TIPS
- Click the logo to switch between the light and dark look.
- Drop WAV, AIFF, FLAC or MP3 files on the window to add them as takes (you pick the bar).
- Drop a lyrics file (.txt, .lrc, .docx) on the window to load your lyrics.
- Click the red button again when you're done, so it doesn't capture while you record other tracks.
- Drag the window's corner to make it bigger or smaller, or use the gear (settings) button.
- The ? button opens the manual.
- If dragging into Cubase doesn't work, click the folder button next to "Drag to DAW": it shows the file, and you can
  drag it from there (or use File > Import > Audio File in Cubase).
