COMP CAPTAIN - HOW TO INSTALL
=============================

This version is M1 (0.2.0): takes in. Record takes with CAPTURE, add audio files, or bring in the takes on a
Cubase track's lanes (ARA). Every take lands on its bar. Lyrics, scores and comping come next; those pages still
show a sample song (marked SAMPLE). On your track it passes your audio through untouched.

WINDOWS (Cubase and other DAWs)
1. Close your DAW.
2. Copy the folder "Comp Captain.vst3" into:  C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
   (Windows may ask for permission: click Continue.)
3. Open your DAW. In Cubase, if it doesn't show up, open Studio > VST Plug-in Manager and click Rescan.
4. Put Comp Captain on a vocal track as an insert effect, then open it.
   "Comp Captain.exe" is the standalone version: double-click it to try it without a DAW.

MAC
1. Copy "Comp Captain.vst3" into  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
   and "Comp Captain.component" into  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
2. Open your DAW and rescan plug-ins if needed.
   (These test builds are not signed yet: if macOS blocks it, right-click > Open, or allow it in
   System Settings > Privacy & Security.)

LINUX
Copy "Comp Captain.vst3" into  ~/.vst3

TRY M1 IN CUBASE (the test for this version)
1. Put Comp Captain on your vocal track (insert). Turn on the track's Monitor button (the speaker).
2. Open Comp Captain, go to the Record page, click the big red button (it glows: armed).
3. Record 5 takes in Cubase (or one cycle recording that loops 5 times). Each pass shows up in the TAKES list,
   with the bar it started on. The Comp page's Song view shows them all on the song's bars.
4. Takes are saved in Documents > Comp Captain (a folder per track). Save the project, close it, reopen it:
   the takes are still listed.
5. ARA: on a track with takes on lanes, select the takes, then Audio > Extensions > Comp Captain.
   Open Comp Captain's Settings (gear), click Copy next to ARA, and paste the report into the chat.

TIPS
- Click the logo to switch between the light and dark look.
- Drop WAV, AIFF, FLAC or MP3 files on the window to add them as takes (you pick the bar).
- Click the red button again when you're done, so it doesn't capture while you record other tracks.
- Drag the window's corner to make it bigger or smaller, or use the gear (settings) button.
- The ? button opens the manual.
