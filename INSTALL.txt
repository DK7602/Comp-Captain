COMP CAPTAIN - HOW TO INSTALL
=============================

This version is M3 (0.4.1): takes, lyrics and scores. Record takes with CAPTURE, add audio files, or bring in the takes
on a Cubase track's lanes (ARA). Every take lands on its bar. Give it your lyrics and it listens to your takes and
lines every line up, so the teleprompter follows your song. Scores and comping come next; those parts still show a
sample song (marked SAMPLE). On your track it passes your audio through untouched.

The "Comp Captain.vst3" folder is bigger now (about 60 MB): it carries the speech model that listens to your takes,
on your computer (nothing is sent anywhere). Copy the whole folder, as before.

WINDOWS (Cubase and other DAWs)
1. Close your DAW.
2. Copy the folder "Comp Captain.vst3" into:  C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
   (Windows may ask for permission: click Continue.)
3. Open your DAW. In Cubase, if it doesn't show up, open Studio > VST Plug-in Manager and click Rescan.
4. Put Comp Captain on a vocal track as an insert effect, then open it.
   "Comp Captain.exe" is the standalone version: double-click it to try it without a DAW. (It borrows the speech
   model from the installed VST3, so copy the VST3 first.)

MAC
1. Copy "Comp Captain.vst3" into  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
   and "Comp Captain.component" into  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
2. Open your DAW and rescan plug-ins if needed.
   (These test builds are not signed yet: if macOS blocks it, right-click > Open, or allow it in
   System Settings > Privacy & Security.)

LINUX
Copy "Comp Captain.vst3" into  ~/.vst3

TRY M3 IN CUBASE (the test for this version)
1. Put Comp Captain on your lead vocal track (insert). Turn on the track's Monitor button (the speaker).
2. Lyrics page: paste the lyrics of the part you'll sing, with [Verse 1] and so on above it.
3. Record page: arm the red button and record 5 or more takes of the same part (a loop in Cubase is easiest).
4. Wait until the Lyrics page has listened to every take ("5 of 5 vocal takes listened to"), then a moment more.
5. Comp page, Line view: every take of the current line, with Pitch, Timing, Clarity, Energy and a total. The
   ticked take is Comp Captain's pick. Use the arrows by the lyric to go through the lines.
6. For each line, decide which take YOU would use, and click it. Do the reasons on the right make sense?
   Does a warning (triangle) really have the problem it names (a pop, clipping, a loud breath)?
7. Review page: the Assistant says how often your picks match Comp Captain's ("on 7 of 9 lines").
   Send that, and anything that looked wrong.
8. Try a Style (Review page, right): Rap doesn't count pitch; Worship / Gospel counts it most.

TIPS
- Click the logo to switch between the light and dark look.
- Drop WAV, AIFF, FLAC or MP3 files on the window to add them as takes (you pick the bar).
- Drop a lyrics file (.txt, .lrc, .docx) on the window to load your lyrics.
- Click the red button again when you're done, so it doesn't capture while you record other tracks.
- Drag the window's corner to make it bigger or smaller, or use the gear (settings) button.
- The ? button opens the manual.
